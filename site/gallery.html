🐒 Nix’s many forms

He has seen things. He has done things. He probably should not be operating your system.

Sixteen portraits of the same chimpanzee. Same hat, same sunglasses, same wings, same dress, same cigar, no explanation.

Catalogue note

One chimpanzee, sixteen situations. The hat, the hibiscus, the aviators, the daisy-print dress, the fairy wings and the cigar are fixed across every plate, because sixteen unrelated chimps are not a mascot.

Nobody knows what Nix is. The placards here say he. The stylesheet that paints them says she. Both are guesses, neither is authoritative, and the gallery has no plans to settle it.

Every sign, book spine and coffee mug in these was drawn to be read, and none of it is legible at this size. Patience (hard mode). Sleep (optional). Reggae & good systems. Open a plate and go along the shelves.
